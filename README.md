# PNRao HR & Retention Analysis Dashboard

### Project Description

Analyzed employee records from 2018 to 2026 to uncover patterns in staffing and turnover. The project involved cleaning and preparing the data, performing exploratory analysis to identify correlations between tenure and attrition, and building an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize the findings.

### Key Insights

- Employees are most likely to leave the company around their 3rd work anniversary, with attrition peaking at 31.82% for this group.
- There is a clear spike in departures between May and September across several departments.
- Marketing currently experiences the highest turnover, while Research & Development shows the highest level of stability.
- Among specific job titles, the Marketing Manager role shows the highest attrition rate at 46.67%.
- Departments like Logistics, Sales, and IT Support maintain a consistent volume of high-performing employees compared to others.

### Technical Skills

- Data Cleaning: Preparing and organizing raw datasets for analysis.
- Data Analysis: Identifying trends and patterns in workforce data.
- Data Visualization: Designing interactive, professional dashboards in Power BI.
- Technology: MySQL, Excel, PowerBI
